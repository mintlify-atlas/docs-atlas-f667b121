## What Makes It Different

<Note>
OptionStrat AI is designed specifically for American-style options with early exercise capabilities, using advanced pricing models that account for dividends and time-varying volatility.
</Note>

### Quantitative Precision

The platform implements multiple pricing models:
- **Black-Scholes-Merton** for European-style approximations and Greeks calculation
- **Binomial trees** (Cox-Ross-Rubinstein) for American option valuation
- **Trinomial trees** for faster convergence and higher accuracy

All models account for dividends, risk-free rates, and volatility shocks to provide realistic valuations.

### AI-Powered Insights

Unlike traditional calculators that just show numbers, OptionStrat AI's LLM analyst:
- Interprets your portfolio Greeks in plain language
- Combines technical analysis with market sentiment and insider trading signals
- Warns you about hidden risks like negative gamma exposure or accelerated theta decay
- Provides actionable recommendations based on your specific position

### Real Market Context

Every analysis incorporates:
- Recent news headlines with sentiment scoring
- Insider trading activity (13F filings via YahooQuery)
- Analyst price targets and recommendations
- Volume and open interest for liquidity assessment

## Architecture Overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="FastAPI Backend">
    Asynchronous Python backend handling data ingestion, quantitative calculations, and AI orchestration
  </Step>
  <Step title="React Frontend">
    Modern React 18 application with Zustand state management for real-time updates and smooth interactions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quantitative Engine">
    NumPy and SciPy-powered calculation engine for Greeks, heatmaps, and pricing models
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Layer">
    LiteLLM router supporting OpenAI and OpenRouter for structured analysis and recommendations
  </Step>
</Steps>
