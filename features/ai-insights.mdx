## IA & Riesgo Tab

### Net Greeks Dashboard

The Greeks panel displays four critical metrics for your entire position:

#### Delta (Δ)

**What it measures**: Directional exposure

```
Positive Delta: Bullish position (benefits from price increase)
Negative Delta: Bearish position (benefits from price decrease)
Near Zero: Delta-neutral position
```

**Color coding**:
- **Green**: Positive delta (long exposure)
- **Red**: Negative delta (short exposure)

<Note>
Delta of +50 means your position moves roughly like 50 shares of stock. Each $1 move in the underlying changes your P&L by ~$50.
</Note>

#### Gamma (Γ)

**What it measures**: Rate of delta change

```
Positive Gamma: Delta increases as stock rises (accelerating profits)
Negative Gamma: Delta works against you (dangerous for short options)
```

**Warning threshold**:
- Gamma < -10: Severe risk alert (expect rapid position changes)

<Warning>
Negative gamma means you're short options. As the stock moves against you, your delta exposure accelerates in the wrong direction.
</Warning>

#### Theta (Θ)

**What it measures**: Time decay per day

```
Positive Theta: You earn money as time passes (credit strategies)
Negative Theta: You lose money as time passes (debit strategies)
```

**Display format**: `+$15.50/dia` or `-$22.30/dia`

<Tip>
Theta accelerates as expiration approaches. A theta of +$15/day means you earn $15 each day, assuming price and volatility stay constant.
</Tip>

#### Vega (V)

**What it measures**: Sensitivity to implied volatility changes

```
Positive Vega: You benefit from IV increases (long options)
Negative Vega: You benefit from IV decreases (short options)
```

A vega of +200 means a 1% increase in IV adds $200 to your position value.

### Risk Score (Peligro Metric)

The danger metric is a 1-10 score calculated from your Greek exposure:

| Score | Risk Level | Indicators |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| **1-3** | Low Risk | Balanced Greeks, modest exposure |
| **4-6** | Moderate Risk | Standard directional or neutral strategies |
| **7-8** | High Risk | Aggressive positioning, negative gamma |
| **9-10** | Extreme Risk | Severe gamma risk, extreme theta decay |

**Color coding**:
- **Green badge (1-6)**: Position is within normal risk parameters
- **Red badge (7-10)**: Position has elevated risk characteristics

<Note>
The score increases based on: |Delta| > 50, Theta < -20, Gamma < -10, and extreme Vega exposure.
</Note>

### Preliminary Verdict (Quick Tips)

The system generates automatic warnings based on your Greeks:

**Theta Alerts**:
```

→ You have negative theta over -20/day

"Ingresas $15.00/dia por primas desgastadas (Theta positiva)."
→ You're collecting time decay (credit strategy)
```

**Delta Alerts**:
```
"Cartera apalancada: Alcista (Delta +75.20)."
→ High bullish exposure

"Cartera apalancada: Bajista (Delta -80.50)."
→ High bearish exposure
```

**Gamma Alerts**:
```
"ALERTA: Gamma negativo severo (Riesgo agudo de volatilidad)."
→ You're short options with dangerous gamma exposure
```

<Warning>
Pay special attention to negative gamma warnings. These indicate you're short options that can move against you quickly.
</Warning>

## Contexto Tab (Fundamental Analysis)

Displays key fundamental metrics for the underlying ticker:

### Business Summary

- Company description (first 300 characters)
- Industry and sector information

### Pricing Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Spot** | Current underlying price |
| **Target Mean** | Average analyst price target |
| **Target Median** | Median analyst price target |
| **Recommendation** | Consensus rating (buy, hold, sell, etc.) |

### Valuation Ratios

| Ratio | What It Means |
|-------|---------------|
| **P/E Forward** | Forward price-to-earnings (expected next 12 months) |
| **P/E Trailing** | Trailing P/E (last 12 months) |
| **Debt to Equity** | Financial leverage ratio |

<Note>
Use analyst targets to inform your strike selection. If the target mean is 10% above current price, consider bullish strategies.
</Note>

## Sentimiento Tab (Market Psychology)

### VADER Finviz Sentiment Score

The sentiment analyzer scrapes recent news headlines and calculates a composite score:

**Score range**: -1.0 (very bearish) to +1.0 (very bullish)

| Score Range | Sentiment | Color |
|-------------|-----------|-------|
| **+0.05 to +1.0** | Bullish | Green |
| **-0.05 to +0.05** | Neutral | Orange |
| **-1.0 to -0.05** | Bearish | Red |

The score is displayed as a percentage (e.g., +15 = +0.15 compound score).

<Tip>
Sentiment is calculated from the last 5 days of news headlines using VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner).
</Tip>

### Recent News Headlines

The panel displays the 4 most recent headlines scraped from Finviz. Examples:

```
"Apple announces record quarterly earnings, beats estimates"
"Concerns over supply chain disruptions impact guidance"
"Analyst upgrades AAPL to Buy with $200 target"
```

### Insider Activity

Two key metrics show insider trading patterns:

**Insider Purchases** (Green)
- Number of purchase transactions in recent period
- Bullish signal when insiders are buying

**Insider Sales** (Red)
- Number of sale transactions in recent period
- Context matters: Routine selling vs. unusual activity

<Note>
Heavy insider buying is often a bullish signal. Heavy selling may indicate concerns, but executives also sell for personal financial planning.
</Note>

### Top Insider Transactions

Displays the 3 most recent insider trades:

```

-25,000 acciones
```

- **Positive shares**: Purchases (green)
- **Negative shares**: Sales (red)

## Advanced Tips

### Interpreting Greek Combinations

**Positive Theta + Negative Gamma**:
```
Typical of: Iron Condors, Credit Spreads
Strategy: You collect time decay but need price to stay in range
Risk: Gamma works against you if price moves sharply
```

**Negative Theta + Positive Gamma**:
```
Typical of: Long Straddles, Calendar Spreads
Strategy: You need price movement or volatility expansion
Risk: Time decay works against you daily
```

**High |Delta| + High |Vega|**:
```
Typical of: Long or short single options
Strategy: Directional play with volatility exposure
Risk: Both price and IV can work against you
```

✅ **Good times to use AI**:
- Before entering a new multi-leg strategy

- When considering adjustments to a losing position

</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

### Greeks Show All Zeros

**Cause**: No legs added to the strategy

**Solution**: Build a position in the Option Builder

### High Risk Score (9-10) on a Simple Spread

**Cause**: Check for extreme gamma or theta values

**Solution**: Review your strikes. Very close-to-ATM short options can have high gamma risk

### Sentiment Shows "No News"

**Cause**: Finviz may not have recent headlines for that ticker

**Solution**: This is common for low-volume tickers. Focus on fundamentals instead

### AI Analysis Returns Generic Response

**Cause**: The LLM needs more context (ticker, market data)

**Solution**: Ensure you've loaded a ticker and market context is available
