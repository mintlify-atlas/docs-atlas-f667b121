## Building Your First Strategy

### 1. Select a Ticker

Enter a ticker symbol in the main search bar to load the option chain. The system will automatically fetch:
- Current spot price
- All available expiration dates
- Option chain with Greeks, volume, and open interest

<Note>
The builder displays the 10 strikes closest to the spot price for optimal visibility. You can switch expiration dates to access different strike ranges.
</Note>

### 2. Understanding the T-Quote Layout

The option chain uses a professional T-quote format:

```
[CALL BID] [CALL ASK] [V/OI] [STRIKE] [V/OI] [PUT BID] [PUT ASK]
```

- **Left side**: Call options (green bid buttons, red ask buttons)
- **Center**: Strike price
- **Right side**: Put options (green bid buttons, red ask buttons)
- **V/OI**: Volume (top) and Open Interest (bottom)

### 3. Adding Legs to Your Strategy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click to Add">
    Click any **Bid** button to SELL that option (credit)
    
    Click any **Ask** button to BUY that option (debit)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Review Active Positions">
    Your active legs appear in the "Opciones Activas" section above the chain
    
    Each leg shows:
    - Action (BUY/SELL)
    - Quantity
    - Type (CALL/PUT/STOCK)
    - Strike price
    - Premium
    - Expiration date
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Remove Legs">
    Click the **✕** button on any active leg to remove it from your strategy
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 4. Adding Stock Positions

For covered calls or protective strategies, use the stock buttons:

- **+100 Acciones (Covered)**: Adds 100 shares long at the current spot price
- **-100 Acciones (Short)**: Adds 100 shares short at the current spot price

<Tip>
Covered calls are popular strategies that combine 100 shares long with 1 short call option. Use the +100 Acciones button, then sell an OTM call.
</Tip>

## Advanced Features

### Multiple Expirations

The expiration dropdown displays all available dates for the ticker. When you change the expiration:

1. The option chain reloads automatically
2. New Greeks and pricing are calculated
3. Your existing legs remain unchanged (they retain their original expirations)

<Warning>
Each leg can have a different expiration date, allowing you to build calendar spreads and diagonal strategies.
</Warning>

### Volume and Open Interest

Each strike displays volume and open interest metrics:

- **Volume**: Number of contracts traded today (white text)
- **Open Interest**: Total open contracts (gray text)

<Note>
Higher volume and open interest typically indicate better liquidity and tighter bid-ask spreads.
</Note>

### Understanding Bid/Ask Colors

- **Green buttons (Bid)**: SELL options - you receive premium (credit)
- **Red buttons (Ask)**: BUY options - you pay premium (debit)

The color coding helps you quickly identify whether you're entering a credit or debit position.

## Common Strategy Examples

### Bull Put Spread (Bullish, Credit)

1. Find a put strike below the current price
2. Click the **Bid** button to SELL the put (receive premium)
3. Find a lower put strike as protection
4. Click the **Ask** button to BUY that put (pay premium)
5. Net result: You receive a credit

### Iron Condor (Neutral, Credit)

1. Build a Bull Put Spread below the current price
2. Build a Bear Call Spread above the current price
3. All four legs should have the same expiration
4. Net result: You receive a credit from both spreads

### Long Straddle (Volatility Play, Debit)

1. Select an ATM strike (near the spot price)
2. Click the **Ask** button on the CALL to BUY it
3. Click the **Ask** button on the PUT (same strike) to BUY it
4. Net result: You pay a debit, profit from large moves in either direction

## What Happens Next?

Once you've built your strategy:

1. The **Payoff Simulator** automatically calculates profit/loss across price ranges
2. The **AI Insights** panel evaluates your Greeks and risk metrics
3. You can adjust parameters like volatility shock and days to expiration

<Tip>
Start simple with 2-leg spreads before building complex 4+ leg strategies. Master the basics first.
</Tip>

## Keyboard Shortcuts

While the Builder is focused:

- Arrow keys navigate through strikes
- Tab cycles between calls and puts
- ESC clears the active strategy
