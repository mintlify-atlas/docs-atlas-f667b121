## Overview

The Strategy Recommender endpoint analyzes real-time options chains and recommends statistically advantageous spread strategies based on your market bias and risk profile. It uses **Theta Gang methodology** to identify optimal trades.

---

## Get Strategy Recommendations

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "http://localhost:8000/api/options/recommend/AAPL?bias=neutral&risk_profile=balanced&min_dte=30&max_dte=60"
```

```python Python
import requests

response = requests.get(
    "http://localhost:8000/api/options/recommend/AAPL",
    params={
        "bias": "neutral",
        "risk_profile": "balanced",
        "min_dte": 30,
        "max_dte": 60
    }
)
recommendations = response.json()
```
</RequestExample>

<ParamField path="ticker" type="string" required>
  Stock ticker symbol (e.g., "AAPL", "SPY", "TSLA")
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="bias" type="string" default="neutral">
  Market direction bias:
  - `bullish` - Expect price to rise
  - `neutral` - Expect price to stay range-bound
  - `bearish` - Expect price to fall
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="risk_profile" type="string" default="balanced">
  Risk tolerance affecting strategy selection:
  - `conservative` - Higher probability of profit, lower credit
  - `balanced` - Moderate risk/reward
  - `aggressive` - Lower probability, higher credit potential
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="min_dte" type="integer" default={30}>
  Minimum days to expiration for option legs. Must be >= 1.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="max_dte" type="integer" default={60}>
  Maximum days to expiration for option legs. Must be 180 or less.
</ParamField>

### Response

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  Status: `"success"` or `"error"`
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="ticker" type="string">
  Ticker symbol in uppercase
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="bias" type="string">
  The market bias used for recommendations
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="risk_profile" type="string">
  The risk profile used
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="spot" type="float">
  Current spot price of the underlying
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="analyzed_expirations" type="array">
  List of expiration dates analyzed (typically one date for consistency)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="recommendations" type="array">
  Array of recommended strategy objects
  
  <Expandable title="Strategy object">
    <ResponseField name="strategy_name" type="string">
      Name of the strategy (e.g., "Bull Put Spread", "Iron Condor", "Credit Call Spread")
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="strategy_type" type="string">
      Category: `"credit"` or `"debit"`
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="expected_credit" type="float">
      Net premium collected (positive) or paid (negative)
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="max_profit" type="float">
      Maximum potential profit
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="max_loss" type="float">
      Maximum potential loss (negative value)
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="probability_of_profit" type="float">

    <ResponseField name="break_even" type="float | array">

    <ResponseField name="risk_reward_ratio" type="float">
      Ratio of max_loss to max_profit (risk/reward)
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="legs" type="array">
      Array of option legs that make up this strategy
      
      <Expandable title="Leg object">
        <ResponseField name="type" type="string">
          `"call"` or `"put"`

        <ResponseField name="action" type="string">
          `"buy"` or `"sell"`
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="strike" type="float">
          Strike price
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="expiration" type="string">
          Expiration date (YYYY-MM-DD)
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="premium" type="float">
          Mid price (bid+ask)/2
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="delta" type="float">
          Delta of this leg
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="theta" type="float">
          Theta of this leg
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="volume" type="integer">
          Trading volume
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="open_interest" type="integer">
          Open interest
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="net_greeks" type="object">
      Net Greeks for the entire strategy
      
      <Expandable title="net_greeks">
        <ResponseField name="delta" type="float">
          Net delta
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="theta" type="float">
          Net theta (daily time decay)

        <ResponseField name="gamma" type="float">
          Net gamma
        </ResponseField>
        
        <ResponseField name="vega" type="float">
          Net vega
        </ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    
    <ResponseField name="description" type="string">
      Human-readable description of the strategy and when to use it
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Strategy Types by Bias

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Neutral">
    Strategies recommended for `bias="neutral"`:
    - **Iron Condor** - Defined risk, moderate credit
    - **Short Strangle** - Undefined risk, higher credit
    - **Iron Butterfly** - Tight profit zone, highest credit
    - **Calendar Spread** - Volatility play
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Bullish">
    Strategies recommended for `bias="bullish"`:
    - **Bull Put Spread** - Credit spread below current price
    - **Cash-Secured Put** - Collect premium, willing to own stock
    - **Poor Man's Covered Call** - Debit spread (LEAPS + short call)
    - **Call Debit Spread** - Limited risk directional play
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Bearish">
    Strategies recommended for `bias="bearish"`:
    - **Bear Call Spread** - Credit spread above current price
    - **Covered Call** - If you own stock
    - **Put Debit Spread** - Limited risk downside play
    - **Short Call Spread** - Credit strategy
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Liquidity Filtering

The recommender only considers liquid options:

<Info>
**Minimum Liquidity Requirements**:
- **Conservative/Balanced**: Open Interest >= 50, Volume >= 10
- **Aggressive**: Open Interest >= 20, Volume >= 5

Illiquid options are filtered out to ensure tradeable recommendations.
</Info>

## Implementation Notes

<Warning>
**Expiration Consistency**: The recommender uses a **single expiration date** for all legs to ensure strategy integrity (e.g., Iron Condor requires all 4 legs to expire on the same date).
</Warning>

<Info>
**DTE Fallback**: If no expirations fall within `min_dte` to `max_dte`, the recommender selects the expiration closest to 45 DTE (the "sweet spot" for theta decay).
</Info>

## Use Cases

- **Strategy Discovery**: Find trades you might not have considered
- **Backtesting Ideas**: Get structured strategies to backtest
- **Education**: Learn different spread constructions
- **Quick Setup**: Copy recommended legs directly into your strategy builder
- **Risk-Adjusted Trading**: Match strategies to your risk tolerance
